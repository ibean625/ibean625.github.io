Unmodified Google Fonts webfonts, self-hosted for the letter composer.
Caveat: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/caveat
LXGW WenKai TC: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/lxgwwenkaitc
WenKai unicode-range subsets only load glyphs used by the text.
