# About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at [Tsinghua University](https://www.sigs.tsinghua.edu.cn/sjyxxyjy/list.htm), advised by Prof. Houde Liu（刘厚德）in the [Tsinghua AI & Robot Lab](https://www.sigs.tsinghua.edu.cn/lhd/list.htm). My research focuses on reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and manipulation models for humanoid robots. I aim to enable robots to perform tasks more accurately and quickly in real-world settings while continually learning and improving. I co-founded [ZERITH](https://zerith.ai/), where I work on humanoid motion control and robot learning. I received my bachelor’s degree in Robotics Engineering from [Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen](http://smea.hitsz.edu.cn/).
