\section{\faFlask\ Research Profile}
My research focuses on \textbf{reinforcement learning}, \textbf{imitation learning}, and \textbf{manipulation models} for humanoid robots. I aim to enable robots to perform tasks more accurately and quickly in real-world settings while continually learning and improving. I co-founded \textbf{ZERITH}, where I work on humanoid motion control and robot learning.
